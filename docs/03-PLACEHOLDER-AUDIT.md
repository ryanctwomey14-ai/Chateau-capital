## Also requires sign-off before launch

**Legal.** `disclosures.html`, `privacy.html` and `terms.html` are structurally complete
templates drafted from standard Regulation D practice. They are not legal advice.
Securities counsel must review them together with every performance-related statement
elsewhere on the site.

**Reg D posture.** The site ships configured for **506(c)**: public thesis content, offering
specifics behind an accreditation gate, with independent third-party verification required
before any subscription. To run **506(b)** instead, remove the Current Offering module from
`invest.html` and rely on the investor list plus a pre-existing substantive relationship.
Nothing else needs to change.
