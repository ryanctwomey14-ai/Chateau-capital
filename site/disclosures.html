Risk factors

An investment of this type is speculative and involves a high degree of risk, including the following. This list is not exhaustive. A complete statement of risk factors appears in the Offering Documents for each offering.

  • Loss of capital. You may lose some or all of your investment. These are leveraged investments in a single asset class.
  • Illiquidity. There is no public market for these interests, no redemption right, and transfers are restricted and generally require sponsor consent.
  • No assurance of distributions. Distributions are targets based on projections, not obligations, and may be reduced, suspended or never paid.
  • Leverage. Properties are financed with debt, which magnifies both gains and losses and may result in foreclosure.
  • Interest rate and refinancing risk. Loans mature. Refinancing may be unavailable or available only on unfavourable terms, and interest rate caps expire.
  • Additional capital. Some structures permit calls for additional capital. Declining to participate may dilute your interest.
  • Reliance on the sponsor. Investors have no role in day-to-day management and depend entirely on the judgement of the manager.
  • Operating cost escalation. Insurance premiums, municipal tax assessments and labour costs have risen materially in several markets and may continue to.
  • Market and concentration risk. Real estate values are cyclical and a portfolio concentrated by geography or asset type is exposed to regional conditions.
  • Tax risk. Tax treatment depends on individual circumstances and on legislation that may change, potentially retroactively.
