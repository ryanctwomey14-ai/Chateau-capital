Chateau Capital AXS1

A multifamily real estate fund built for long-term wealth creation through institutional-quality apartment investments.

AXS1 targets recession-resilient multifamily assets: well-located workforce communities whose demand holds up when conditions tighten. Every opportunity runs through the same comprehensive underwriting process before it reaches the fund, which is how we identify the deals with genuine risk-adjusted return potential and decline the ones that only work on optimism.

Investors receive quarterly cash distributions, participate in a targeted 15 to 20% annualised return, and benefit from the tax advantages of real estate ownership through depreciation.

What we mean by recession-resilient

A word worth defining, because everyone in this category uses it and almost nobody says what it means.

01

Housing people cannot easily trade down from

Workforce communities are already the affordable option in their submarket. When budgets tighten, residents renew rather than move up, and the households moving down are moving toward us rather than away.

02

Income spread across hundreds of leases

No single tenant matters. A six percent vacancy still collects ninety-four percent of the rent, every month, which is a fundamentally different risk profile from a single-tenant asset.

03

Debt that cannot force a sale

Fixed or fully capped, with maturity beyond the planned hold. The sponsors who failed between 2022 and 2024 were rarely beaten by their buildings. They were beaten by their loans.

What AXS1 does for you

Quarterly Cash distributions

Targeted quarterly by direct deposit, with a statement showing exactly how the figure was calculated. Distributions are targets and may be reduced or suspended.

15–20% Targeted annualised return

A projection based on the fund's underwriting assumptions, not a promise. Your capital is at risk and returns may be lower, or negative.

K-1 Tax-advantaged ownership

Depreciation on the buildings flows through to you and can offset a portion of the cash you receive. Passive activity loss rules apply. See how this works.

Who AXS1 is for

  • Accredited investors, verified independently before subscribing
  • Investors who can commit capital for five to seven years
  • Those seeking income and tax efficiency alongside long-term growth
  • Investors diversifying away from public equities
  • People who would rather delegate to operators than manage property

What you are accepting

  • Your capital is illiquid. There is no public market and no redemption right.
  • You could lose some or all of your investment.
  • Distributions are targeted, not obligated, and can be reduced or suspended.
  • Returns depend on the sponsor's execution. Operator risk is the key risk.
  • Some structures permit calls for additional capital. Read that section closely.
