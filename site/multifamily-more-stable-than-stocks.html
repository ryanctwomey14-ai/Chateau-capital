What Makes Multifamily More Stable Than Stocks

Not a claim about returns. A claim about where the value comes from, and what can move it.

David Snider / 9 July 2025 / 3 min read

Stability is a word used loosely in this category. It is worth being precise about what is actually being claimed, and what is not.

Value comes from different places

A share price reflects what buyers and sellers believe a company is worth at a given moment. An apartment community's value reflects the income it produces. One is a matter of sentiment and can move overnight. The other is a matter of collections and moves with leases.

What that means in practice

  • No daily repricing. Private real estate is not marked to market by a screen, so it does not swing on headlines.
  • Income from many sources. Hundreds of leases rather than one revenue line.
  • Demand that persists. Housing is the last expense a household cuts.
  • Costs you can see. Operating expenses are budgeted, reviewed weekly and largely within the operator's control.

What stability does not mean

It does not mean the value cannot fall. It can, and between 2022 and 2024 it did for a great many owners. It does not mean distributions are assured. And it does not remove the largest risk in private real estate, which is the operator.

What the structure offers is fewer sources of sudden movement, not an absence of risk.

The risk that replaces market risk

In private real estate you exchange market volatility for operator dependence. That is a real trade, not a free upgrade. Diligence the sponsor at least as carefully as the property.
