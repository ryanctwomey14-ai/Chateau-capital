Founder

Dave Snider

Founder, Manager

I was born into a family deeply rooted in ministry and education. My parents, both committed to serving others, raised me and my three brothers in modest housing that was often in need of repair. From an early age I was hands-on, helping my father fix plumbing, heating and structural problems, and working alongside my uncle, a skilled mason. I saw firsthand how resourcefulness and hard work could turn a neglected property into a valuable one.

That foundation shaped my path. By the time I was in seminary I was already investing in foreclosures, starting with one property at a time and then scaling to several projects at once. I graduated from UNC Chapel Hill with a BA and a concentration in Economics, while building a top-five team nationally in the insurance industry with NML as College Unit Director. I then went to Southeastern Seminary in Wake Forest, North Carolina.

While there I learned to acquire distressed and estate properties, and to finish homes left incomplete by builders, turning them into profitable investments. My journey was never only about real estate. It was about people. Whether it was homeowners, tenants or team members, I built my career around creating value and making sure the outcome worked for everyone involved.

Our Story

Why we called it Chateau

Dave initially focused on acquiring, renovating and beautifying starter and affordable houses. Within a year he began expanding into managing rental properties as well, as his parents had, and I have always handled the bookkeeping and accounting.

The market for starter homes and affordable housing was the strongest then, and it still is in any economy. We wanted each house, however ugly it was when we bought it, to become someone's chateau: an esteemed, admired home they loved, could raise a family in, and could build a life in.

We later founded Chateau Street Stays LLC to give renters a better and better-managed experience across Western and Central North Carolina and coastal South Carolina. Those properties host over 3,000 guests a year. We founded Triad Chateau to provide better rental experiences and better accounting in the Triad of North Carolina. We founded Chateau Holdings LLC to oversee the Chateau companies. We then founded Chateau Capital LLC, doing business as Chateau Funds, to use private investor capital to fund better multifamily properties and deliver returns and tax benefits to investors, so they can earn more and worry less.

The properties we analyse, acquire, improve and manage are typically within a six-hour radius of where we live. Occasionally we hear of opportunities outside that radius, and we will analyse them if they would genuinely benefit investors.

We do our best to improve the communities we invest in, resident retention and occupancy, the quality of life of the people living there, and the returns we deliver to the investors who make it possible.

How decisions actually get made

Three rules of governance that sit behind every acquisition.

01

Underwriting is separated from acquisitions

The person who negotiates a deal is not the person who models it. Enthusiasm is useful in a negotiation and dangerous in a spreadsheet, so we keep the two functions in different hands.

02

Any member of the committee can stop a deal

Approval requires consensus. A single unresolved objection is enough to end an acquisition, and no one is required to justify caution.

03

We invest our own capital on your terms

The general partner co-invests 3 to 5 percent of the equity in every offering, on identical terms to limited partners. We take the same outcome you do.
