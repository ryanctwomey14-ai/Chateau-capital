How investing with us works

Four steps, no obligation at any of them, and a clear answer about fit before anyone asks you for money.

The Path

From first conversation to funded

You are not committed to anything until step four, and we will say so at every step before it.

Introduction

Twenty minutes. Your objectives, our approach, and an honest answer about whether this fits. No pitch.

Verification

Independent third-party confirmation of accredited status. Required by regulation, handled by a specialist service.

Deal room

Full offering documents, the financial model, the property condition reports and a live webinar with the deal team.

Subscribe and fund

Sign the subscription documents electronically and wire. This is the first point at which you are committed.

Are you an accredited investor?

Our current offerings are made under Rule 506(c) of Regulation D, which limits participation to accredited investors. This takes about fifteen seconds and nothing is recorded.

Accreditation self-check

This self-check is for your information only. It is not a determination of your status, no answer is stored, and it does not constitute an offer. Accredited investor status must be independently verified before any subscription can be accepted.
