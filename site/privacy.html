Privacy policy

Template for counsel review. A working draft covering the data this site actually collects. It must be reviewed against the firm's real vendor stack, data retention practices and applicable state privacy laws before launch.

We collect the minimum we need to have a useful conversation with you, and we do not sell it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. Name, email address, telephone number, investment timeline and anything you write in a message field when you download the investor guide, join the investor list, request a conversation or book a call.

Information collected automatically. IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages viewed and time on page, gathered through standard web analytics.

Information from verification partners. If you proceed toward an investment, an independent verification service may confirm your accredited investor status. Documentation supplied to that service is governed by its own privacy terms.

We do not ask for and you should not send financial account numbers, tax identification numbers or other sensitive identifiers through this website.

How we use it

  • To send you material you requested, such as the investor guide
  • To contact you about a conversation you requested
  • To notify investors on the list about new offerings and commentary
  • To meet regulatory obligations, including accreditation verification and record keeping
  • To understand which pages are useful so that we can improve them

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent or trade our investor list. We do not share your information with other sponsors, and we do not use it for advertising unrelated to Chateau Capital.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who need it to operate the business, under contracts requiring them to protect it. These currently include our customer relationship management platform, our email service provider, our web analytics provider, our accreditation verification partner, our investor portal and fund administrator, and our professional advisers. We may also disclose information where required by law or regulation.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies for basic functionality and for aggregate analytics. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may then work less well. Where required, a consent banner will be presented and non-essential cookies will not be set unless you accept them.

Email

Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes you from marketing email. If you are an existing limited partner, we will still send communications we are obliged to send about your investment.

Retention

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards. Investor records are kept for the period required by applicable securities, tax and anti-money-laundering rules, which is generally longer.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and access to personal information is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to opt out of certain sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. To make a request, write to the address below. We will verify your identity before acting.

Children

This site is intended for adults evaluating investments. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date below and, where the change is material, tell investors on our list.
