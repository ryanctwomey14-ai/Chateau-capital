What We Do

We buy apartment communities.
Then we run them properly.

Chateau Capital acquires stabilised workforce apartment communities, the kind of well-located, unglamorous housing that stays occupied in good markets and in bad ones. Investors participate through a limited partnership and own a proportional share of the real estate. We do the rest.

Acquire

We source, underwrite and close the asset, and we invest our own capital alongside yours in every deal.

Improve

Units are renovated on natural turnover, never by displacing a paying resident, at a cost per unit already tested against achievable rent.

Operate

Weekly performance reviews with on-site management against a fixed budget. Returns in this business are earned in operations, not at the closing table.

Distribute and exit

Cash flow is distributed quarterly as targeted. We hold for five to seven years and sell when the market rewards selling.

What that means for you

Four changes to your position, each a direct consequence of the work described above.

You become an owner without becoming an operator

You hold a share of physical buildings with a rent roll, and you do none of the work. No tenant calls, no turnover, no contractors, no plumbing emergency at eleven at night. The distance between owning real estate and running it is the reason this firm exists.

Income you do not have to manage

Cash flow from hundreds of leases, distributed quarterly as targeted. One vacancy inside a 200-unit community is a rounding error rather than an event.

Capital in something real

A building, a rent roll and a physical address, rather than another position correlated to the equities already dominating your balance sheet.

Value that can be built, not waited for

Apartments are priced on the income they produce. Raising that income through better operations creates equity, rather than leaving you waiting for a market to hand it to you.

Income the tax code treats differently from your salary

Depreciation on the building flows through to you on a K-1 and can offset a portion of the cash you receive, which is why real estate income and wage income are not taxed alike. The effect depends entirely on your circumstances, and it is a conversation for your own CPA rather than for a website.

What we will, and will not, buy

If an opportunity falls outside these, we pass, regardless of how good the story is.

  • 100 to 400 units. Large enough to carry full-time on-site staff, small enough to sit below the biggest institutional buyers.
  • 1985 to 2015 vintage. Past the worst construction eras, young enough to avoid whole-system replacement.
  • Class B workforce housing. Renters by necessity, not by lifestyle. Demand is least elastic where housing is least discretionary.
  • Supply-constrained growth markets. Positive migration, diverse employers, and a forward construction pipeline small enough that our rents are not competing with a brand-new building offering three months free.
  • Fixed or fully capped debt. No uncapped floating-rate exposure. Ever.
  • Coverage on day one. The asset must service its debt from the first month on in-place income, not on projected rents.

Every number on this page follows from that list, which is why it is worth reading closely.

This is not for everyone, deliberately

A good fit if you

  • Are an accredited investor
  • Can commit capital for five to seven years without needing it back
  • Want income and tax efficiency more than you want excitement
  • Would rather delegate to operators than manage property yourself
  • Are diversifying away from a portfolio concentrated in public equities
  • Value a sponsor who tells you the truth over one who tells you a story

Not a fit if you

  • May need access to this capital within five years
  • Are looking for guaranteed or principal-protected returns
  • Are trying to make up a retirement shortfall quickly
  • Want to approve individual decisions or properties
  • Are not yet accredited. We will keep you on the educational list instead.

If you read the right-hand column and nothing there disqualified you, the next step is a conversation.
