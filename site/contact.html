Let's have a conversation

Twenty minutes with a partner. We will ask about your objectives, explain how we invest, and tell you honestly if we are not the right fit.

What happens on the call

01

We ask about you

Your objectives, your timeline, what else you hold, and what you are actually trying to solve. Usually about eight minutes.

02

We explain how we invest

The buy box, the debt policy, the fee structure and the risks. You can interrupt with hard questions at any point. Most people do.

03

We give you a straight answer on fit

If your timeline, liquidity needs or objectives point somewhere other than here, we will say so. That happens often and it costs us nothing.

What will not happen

No slide deck. No urgency tactics. No follow-up sequence unless you ask to be added to the investor list. If you decide against it, that is a perfectly good outcome and we will not chase you.

If you are inside a 1031 exchange window or have proceeds sitting from a sale, call rather than write.
