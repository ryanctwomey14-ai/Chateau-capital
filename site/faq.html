Money and returns

Distributions are targeted quarterly by direct deposit, typically beginning twelve to eighteen months after closing once the business plan is underway. They are targets rather than obligations and may be reduced or suspended if the property requires the cash or a lender requires it to be retained.

A preferred return is the annual return limited partners receive on their capital before the sponsor participates in any profit. Ours is 7 percent and it is cumulative, which means that if it is not paid in a given year it accrues and must be caught up before we receive anything.

This distinction matters more than the headline percentage. A non-cumulative 8 percent is worth less to you than a cumulative 7 percent, because an unpaid year simply disappears.

The complete fee table and waterfall are published on the strategy page. In summary: an acquisition fee at closing, an asset management fee charged on collected revenue, a disposition fee at sale, and a profit split that we do not receive until you have received your capital back plus the preferred return.

Each offering states its own targets, and those targets are projections rather than promises. We would rather you evaluate the assumptions behind a target than the target itself, so the model is made available in the deal room and we will walk you through it line by line. Any sponsor who leads with a return number and resists showing the assumptions is telling you something important.

Tax

You receive a Schedule K-1 each year reporting your share of the partnership's income, loss and depreciation. Because depreciation is a non-cash deduction, it frequently offsets a large share of the cash you actually receive, so distributions are often taxed at less than their face value in the early years.

Profit on sale is generally treated as long-term capital gain, subject to depreciation recapture. Your outcome depends entirely on your own circumstances. This is not tax advice.

We commit to a published delivery date each year and tell you early if we are at risk of missing it. If a K-1 will be late, we say so in advance so that you can file an extension rather than discover the problem in April.

1031 exchanges

Not into a standard limited partnership interest. A 1031 exchange requires you to acquire like-kind real property, and an LP interest is treated as a security rather than as real estate.

Exchanges are generally accommodated through a Delaware Statutory Trust or a tenant-in-common structure. These must be arranged in advance, so the single most important thing is to speak to us before you close the sale of your relinquished property, not after.
